💡 Helpful Information

📌 Main Tools
• Calendar: Monthly overview of activities and notes.
• Daily Notifications: Scheduled reminders (9:00am, 5:45pm).
• Metrics: Track your weekly/monthly progress and goals.
• Journal: Daily reflections and weekly reviews.
• Personal Tracker: Skills development and progress tracking.
⚡ Quick Tips
Notes & Checklist: Type normally for checklist items, or start with "- " for a note.
Tasks: Completed items auto-clear at midnight.
Calendar: Weekends appear darker. Holidays are marked in red.
💾 Data Storage
Everything saves to browser storage locally. Data syncs to cloud backup automatically. No internet required once loaded. All data is private and personal.
